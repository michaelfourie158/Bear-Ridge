Who we are

Two decades of moving bulk

Bear Ridge Holdings is a transport and logistics company based in Nelspruit, South Africa. We specialise in moving coal, chrome, cement, fertilizer and timber to destinations across the region, with services designed around what each client needs.

Founded in 2001, we've built our name on efficient, secure transport. Our team is dedicated to making sure your cargo reaches its destination safely and on time.

  • Safe handling on every load
  • On time delivery, every run
  • Cross-border to Maputo and Ressano
